From download to discovery

How to install and use it.

GET YOUR ACCOUNT

Sign in & request access

Sign in with ChatGPT, submit your name and contact details, then download the extension ZIP. Access requires approval.

ADD TO CHROME

Extract & install

Extract the ZIP. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, select Load unpacked and choose the extracted extension folder.

CONNECT YOUR EXTENSION

Sign in & get started

Use Sign in inside the extension. Once approved, your connected extension unlocks automatically while online.

After installation: Open Google Maps, search for the type of business and location you need, then open Maps Lead Finder from Chrome’s extensions menu. Save the available records and export your list when you’re ready.
First-time setup: Keep the extracted folder on your computer. Removing it can break an unpacked extension. You need an internet connection for access verification.
Before you start

A few useful details.

Can I use it immediately after downloading?

You can install the extension after downloading. Its research tools unlock after your access request is approved.

Do I need to copy a license key?

No. Access uses your signed-in account. Sign in through the extension to connect it.

Will every business have an email or phone number?

No. Results depend on the information available for each business. Contact details and lookup results are not guaranteed.

Does it work on mobile?

This release is designed for desktop Chrome using Load unpacked installation.
