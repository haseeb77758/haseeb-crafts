Install in Chrome

1. Download & extract

Use Sign in & Download, submit your access request, download the ZIP and extract it. Keep the maps-lead-finder folder.

2. Load the extension

Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select that folder.

3. Sign in & connect

Click Sign in in the extension popup. After owner approval, click Connect extension in the portal, then reload Google Maps.

Internet verification is required. Access changes are checked online; an open Maps panel rechecks within 60 seconds. Download records show registered download requests, not proof that a file finished downloading.
