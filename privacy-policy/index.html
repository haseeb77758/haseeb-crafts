About this website policy

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you visit haseebcrafts.com, use its browser-based design tools, or send an enquiry through the website. Haseeb Crafts is operated by Abdul Haseeb in Pakistan. This policy covers this website; separate client websites, standalone apps and browser extensions have their own data practices.

Information you choose to share

The contact form asks for your name, email address, selected service and project details. You may also provide a phone number and budget. We use the information you send to respond to your enquiry, understand your requirements and discuss the work you requested. Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or other sensitive information in these fields.

Previewing a brief displays it in your browser. Copying a brief places it on your device’s clipboard, and downloading it saves a file on your device. These actions do not themselves submit the brief to us.

Email, FormSubmit and WhatsApp

Choosing “Send inquiry via Email” submits the form information to FormSubmit, a third-party service configured to forward enquiries to hmm4405930@gmail.com. The form requests a CAPTCHA verification step. FormSubmit and any verification service may process technical information and use cookies under their own privacy notices. A submission may require verification before delivery.

WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp with a prepared message containing the details you entered. Opening that link passes the prepared text to WhatsApp; you must press Send there to deliver the message to us. Email links may open your email application with a prepared subject or message. These services handle information under their own policies, including WhatsApp’s Privacy Policy.

Design tools and saved designs

Try It Live includes website previews, brand and logo tools, a cost calculator, page builder, poster maker and guided Design Assistant. These tools process your entries in your browser. The Design Assistant uses predefined responses rather than sending questions to an external generative AI model. Photographs used in previews may still load from an external provider as explained below.

When you select Save Design, information such as the project or business name, selected category, colours, layout, sections and estimate is saved in local storage in that browser profile. Up to 20 designs can be retained until you delete them or clear website data. Saved designs are not automatically sent to Haseeb Crafts. Choosing to request a quote prepares selected details for email or WhatsApp.

Use the Delete control beside a saved design, or clear this website’s data in your browser settings, to remove locally saved designs. Exported HTML, JSON, images and brief files remain in the location where you download them. Clearing website data does not delete those downloads. Other people using the same device and browser profile may be able to access locally saved information.

Hosting and external resources

Hosting and network providers may process technical request information, including your IP address, browser details, requested URLs and request times, to deliver and protect the website.

The website loads Google Fonts. Try It Live previews load photographs from Unsplash, and portfolio thumbnails may load through WordPress.com’s screenshot service. Requests to these providers can disclose your IP address, browser information and, depending on browser settings, a referring page URL. Their processing is governed by their own policies: Google, Unsplash and Automattic / WordPress.com.

Pinterest inspiration searches and portfolio links open external websites. Those websites may collect information when you visit them and may process it outside your country. This policy does not control their data practices.

Cookies, storage and search visibility

The website tools use local storage for the saved designs described above. Third-party services you access, including form submission and verification services, may use cookies or similar technologies. You can control cookies, storage and external resource loading through your browser; blocking them may affect forms, fonts, images or saving designs.

Google Search Console verification helps establish ownership of the website for search reporting. A verification tag is not an analytics tracking script. If website advertising, analytics or other data processing changes, this page will be updated to describe the actual setup.

Correspondence, sharing and retention

Information you send through email or WhatsApp can remain in our correspondence and the relevant service provider’s systems so that we can respond to your request and manage any resulting work. Retention depends on the enquiry, ongoing work and any applicable recordkeeping needs; this website does not set an automatic deletion period for those messages.

Information passes to the providers needed for the communication or feature you choose, as described above. Information may also need to be retained or disclosed to meet applicable legal obligations or address a dispute. Local saved designs remain under the controls of your browser and device.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can browse without submitting an enquiry, avoid external links and choose whether to save designs. To ask about information you have sent us, request a correction or ask for deletion, email hmm4405930@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify the request and identify the relevant correspondence. Some information may need to be retained for ongoing work, legal obligations or a dispute.

A request to Haseeb Crafts does not automatically remove information held independently by another provider or files stored on your device. Use the provider’s own controls for its records and your browser or file manager for local copies.

Security and policy updates

No website, device or communication service can guarantee complete security. Take care with information entered on shared devices and sent through external services. We will revise this page when the website’s data practices change and update the date shown above.

Contact about this website

For privacy questions about haseebcrafts.com, contact Abdul Haseeb at hmm4405930@gmail.com. You can also use the contact options on the Contact page.
