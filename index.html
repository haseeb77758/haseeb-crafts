COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQs

Clear answers before we start building.

More questions →

What services do you offer?

Website development, WordPress and Elementor builds, motion graphics, video editing, branding and identity (including graphic design) and social media design. Each has its own page under Services.

Will my website work on phones and tablets?

Layouts are designed to be responsive, so they adapt from phone to desktop. Which devices and browsers to check is agreed at the start of the project.

How does the process work?

Four steps: Discover (goals and references), Design (direction and layouts), Create (the build or production) and Deliver (checks, hand-over and files).

What do you need from me to get started?

A short description of your goals and audience, any logo or brand assets, the text and images you already have, a few references you like, and a deadline or budget range if you have one.

How do revisions work?

The number of revision rounds is agreed in the project scope. Changes beyond that scope are discussed with you before any extra work begins.

How long will my project take?

It depends on scope, how ready your content is and how quickly feedback arrives. Timing is agreed per project after the brief has been reviewed, and no fixed timelines are promised in advance.
