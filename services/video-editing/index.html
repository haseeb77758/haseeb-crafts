Video Editing

Every frame, thoughtfully edited.

Overview

Editing for footage you already have: pacing, cuts, titles, captions, sound balance and colour treatment, shaped for the platform where the video will be seen.

Pricing and timing are agreed per project once the scope is clear.

Discuss Your Project

Deliverables

  • Edited video from your supplied footage
  • Titles, captions and simple graphics
  • Audio balancing and music placement
  • Colour treatment for a consistent look
  • Exports for web or social formats

Exact deliverables are confirmed in the project scope.

Process

  1. Review footage, references and the goal for the video
  2. Assemble a first cut
  3. Refine pacing, graphics and sound with your notes
  4. Export and deliver the final files
