Website Development

Websites built around your ideas.

Overview

Clear, responsive websites designed around what you want people to understand and do. Layout, content structure, visual design and build come together as one considered piece of work.

Pricing and timing are agreed per project once the scope is clear.

Discuss Your Project

Deliverables

  • Page structure and wireframes
  • Responsive visual design
  • Built, tested pages for phone, tablet and desktop
  • Basic on-page SEO setup (titles, descriptions, headings)
  • Hand-over notes for what was built

Exact deliverables are confirmed in the project scope.

Process

  1. Agree goals, audience and the pages needed
  2. Sketch structure and design the key screens
  3. Build and refine the pages with your feedback
  4. Test, adjust and hand over the finished site
